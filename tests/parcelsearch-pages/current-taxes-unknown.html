Please Be Aware
  1. Information Posting Timeline: Current year tax information will be posted on this screen as it becomes available. Ownership information will be posted in the Spring, property values will be posted in June and tax amounts due will be posted in mid-October.

  2. Prior-Year Data Is Frozen: If you use the 'View Prior Years' selection box below, the prior year information is frozen for illustration purposes to show the year end's status for the year you choose. This includes the 'balance' due amount.

This delinquent balance will not be transferred to the 'Delinquent Taxes' screen (see pull-down menu item above) until February 1st of next year.
